# Neevii Ink 0.1.22

- Smoother Watercolour spreading, drying and blending between painted strokes, with independent **Wet-on-wet** and **Fresh-paper dryness** controls.
- Better Linux Watercolour performance and a clearer scrollbar gutter so expanded right-hand panels remain usable.
- Faster, more responsive Watercolour Smudge mixes existing colour without adding the foreground colour, with fewer pauses and square update artefacts.
- Paint with **Watercolour Fine Soft Bristles** and trajectory-following tip rotation, with editable angles and reset controls.
- Erasing and smudging Oil or Acrylic now updates the paint relief consistently. Mixed-media edits and layer expansion preserve existing paint and paper texture.
- Correct compact-layer alpha selections and clipped adjustments; retain excluded Merge Selected children and duplicate complete layer groups.
- Control on-demand ComfyUI from the header, refresh Image Browser files and previews, and keep your chosen colour-picker layout even before opening a document.

Known limitation: very fast Watercolour Smudge strokes can still take about one second to finish after pen-up.
